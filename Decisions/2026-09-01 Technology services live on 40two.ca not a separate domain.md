# Technology services live on 40two.ca, not a separate domain

## Context
40two.ca is positioned around proposal management. Technology consulting is becoming a renewed focus and needed a public home. The alternative was a separate 40TwoTechnologies.ca site.

## Decision
Add a top-level "Technology" section to 40two.ca (landing page + service pages) and add Mike to the About page. Public lineup: Shopify, WordPress hosting & maintenance, custom web apps, fractional CTO/strategy. Hold back M365/security and AI/automation for now.

## Consequences
One brand, one site to maintain, shared domain authority. Technology CTAs route to Mike's Calendly rather than Amber's.
