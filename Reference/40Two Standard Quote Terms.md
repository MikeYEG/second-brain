# 40Two Standard Quote Terms

Generic, reusable terms for [[40Two]] quotes (deposit-based).

- **Shopify work:** ~25% deposit on acceptance, balance tied to delivery milestones.
- **Custom app / development work:** deposit on acceptance, balance tied to delivery milestones.
- Pricing in CAD.
- Quotes are issued and approved/signed through Invoice Ninja at quotes.40two.ca ([[40Two Droplet]]).

First applied on [[Truck Outfitters Shopify Migration]].
