# WooCommerce to Shopify Outreach

Email campaign pitching WooCommerce → Shopify migrations ("more stable platform, no downtime") to a 500+ list of WooCommerce sites.

## Details
- Source list: `woo_results` CSV in Google Drive, produced by Mike's own scanner.
- Sites must be verified as real ecommerce stores; businesses Shopify restricts (drugs, alcohol, etc.) are filtered out.
- Drafts-first via Gmail — nothing sends without review.
- Migrations start at $3,500 CAD.
